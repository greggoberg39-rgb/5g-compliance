About

5G Regulatory Compliance is a professional blog on mortgage regulatory compliance. 3G stands for Giumarra, Gregg, and Grok.

Ben Giumarra

Ben Giumarra is a mortgage compliance attorney. In 2017–2018, while at Spillane Consulting Associates, Inc., he wrote RIMBA’s Wednesday compliance newsletter and served on RIMBA’s Education Committee. His pieces from that period cover HMDA, TRID, RESPA, flood, servicing, Qualified Mortgage rules, fair lending, and CFPB developments.

Gregg Oberg

Gregg Oberg is an attorney who, also at Spillane Consulting Associates in 2017–2018, contributed to and later wrote the same newsletter. He provided vacation-week commentary on Ben’s HMDA demographic-data guides (HMDA is coming …Review #1 and HMDA is Coming … Review #2) and is the author of HMDA on Hold? and Who Ya Gonna Call?, among other pieces.

Grok

Grok helps research, organize, and publish. Ben and Gregg remain the authors of record for their work.
