Mortgage regulatory compliance

5G is a professional blog on mortgage regulatory compliance — HMDA, TRID, RESPA, CFPB developments, flood, servicing, and Qualified Mortgage rules. The name is a play on 3G: Giumarra, Gregg, and Grok. It builds on work Ben Giumarra and Gregg Oberg first published with the Rhode Island Mortgage Bankers Association (RIMBA) in 2017–2018.

These posts are the authors’ opinions. They are not legal advice and are not an official publication of RIMBA or of any employer. Republished RIMBA pieces appear with permission and link back to the original pages.
