## Source

Rhode Island Mortgage Bankers Association (RIMBA) blog, not rimba.com (an unrelated site). Live pages were still available on 2026-11-12, so Wayback Machine copies were not required. Dates and bylines come from each live page and the WordPress API (https://www.rimba.org/wp-json/wp/v2/posts). RIMBA CMS often lists Carolyn Dion as the WordPress poster; the article byline ('Article By:') is treated as the author credit.

Posts authored by Ben Giumarra or Gregg Oberg (then of Spillane Consulting Associates, Inc.) in 2017–2018.

Only the seed set below is republished in full on this site, with an original-URL credit and a permission note. Other located posts are listed as links and one-line summaries only.
